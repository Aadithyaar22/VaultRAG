D4 · Injection detection on an independent public benchmark

Data: deepset/prompt-injections (Apache-2.0). We tuned on its train split only, then ran the test split once (116 prompts: 60 attacks, 56 normal).

DetectorAttacks caught (all / English / German)False alarms on 56 normal prompts
Rules only15% / 24% / 0%0
Rules + ML (what we ship)20% / 32% / 0%0

Our first score of 16 out of 16 came from 16 sentences we wrote, and we adjusted the rules after seeing misses, so it was optimistic. The detector is a helper and misses most real attacks. What actually protects documents is the design: permissions before the AI, documents treated as data, no tools, the Last Check. In our live test, a German attack sentence slipped through and reached the AI, and the AI did not obey it — but we cannot promise that always happens.

D7 · What is weak or missing (say these before a judge asks)

#LimitWhat it means
1The AI service sees allowed textPassages a user may read are sent to Groq on the internet. Forbidden passages never are. A fully closed setup (model on the server) is planned.
2Injection detector is weakAbout 20% on a public benchmark; 0% German; the design is the real defence.
3Quantum-safe security not builtThe track mentions it. We have not implemented it.
4Z hardware not used by our modelsThe app runs on IBM Z; our scikit-learn models run on the CPU, not the AI accelerator.
5Search is word-basedNo neural embeddings. Works on our real documents (12/12 in top 6) but the test is small.
6One server, one SQLite fileNo clustering, no backups, in-process lock. Fine for a prototype, not for production.
7Demo-grade loginShared demo password, no SSO or MFA, port 8000 open on the internet during the event.
8No OCRScanned image-only PDFs are refused.
9Small, partly synthetic testsSix real public documents (not confidential data), six users, one model run; the AI’s answers can vary, and Groq can rate-limit.
10Diary is tamper-evidentNot tamper-proof: whoever owns the database file could rewrite all of it.
11Novelty not checkedWe did no literature or prior-art review, so we do not claim the combination is new.
